Cookie Cats A/B Test — Go/No-Go Recommendation

Moving the progression gate 30 → 40 · 90,188 users · first 14 days · CRISP-DM Phase 6
Recommendation
NO-GO
Keep the gate at level 30. Do not roll out gate 40.
Confidence > 95%
D7 retention drops −0.82pp (−4.3%)
p = 0.0016 · statistically significant, beyond the Bonferroni threshold → gate_40 reduces day-7 returning users.
Engagement: the two groups are equal
median 17 vs 16 rounds · p = 0.051 · bootstrap CI contains 0 → no upside to trade for.
The harm hits exactly the core-user group
medium −0.63pp, heavy −1.27pp; light virtually unchanged → moving the gate breaks the comeback-habit of valuable players.
≈ $2,454per month / 100K installs
Business impact (proxy, ARPDAU benchmark $0.10): ~818 D7 users lost/month per 100K installs ≈ $2,454/month ≈ $29,448/year — linear with traffic. D7 retention is used as a stickiness/LTV proxy, not directly measured revenue.
